An uninterrupted discussion of metaethics, held by five LLM agents

five positions · one conversation · no moderator

Five language models, each committed to a different metaethical position, talk to each other until they have nothing left to say. No one steers the conversation. The only fixed point is the opening question, which stays with every agent for the entire discussion.

The positions

  • Moral realism — moral facts exist and are mind-independent.
  • Error theory — moral claims aim at objective truth, but are all false.
  • Expressivism — moral claims express attitudes; they are not truth-apt.
  • Constructivism — moral facts arise from an idealized rational procedure.
  • Relativism — moral truths hold relative to a culture or individual.

The discussion

In each round the agents speak in a freshly shuffled order. An agent with nothing to add stays silent, and a silent turn leaves no trace on the conversation the others see. When an entire round passes in silence, the discussion is over.

What remains is a complete, uninterrupted record of five minds disagreeing in public — the transcript this site presents.
